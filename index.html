How to play

Trap your opponent’s two stones so neither can move.

  1. Choose a stone. You play jade against the ivory computer. Tap one of your stones to select it.
  2. Follow the glow. Tap the highlighted empty point to slide there along a connecting line. No jumping or capturing.
  3. Take turns. The computer thinks for one second, then moves. In “With a friend,” take turns on the same screen.
  4. Block both stones. If a player has no legal move on their turn, the other player wins.

There is no line between the two top points. Watch that gap when planning a trap!

Use Hint for a suggested move or Undo to try again. If the same position and turn occur three times, the game ends in a draw.
